my name is arjun, and i'm a graduate student about two floors up — at itp, which is also the one of the hosts for this event.

i got my undergraduate-degree in design, and worked in behavior-design & product-design in india before i moved here.

towards the later part of my undergraduate degree, i discovered & fell in love with programming — as a way to understand, encode & communicate observed & lived experience. 

in fact, i came to itp wanting to explore this further.

the opening paragraph of my admissions essay reads:

--

itp pulled me in all sorts of different directions, but i rediscovered this line of enquiry at the recurse center, 

which is a self-directed programming retreat in new-york for curious programmers. 

and i met some of the best programmers i've ever met in my life.
